- **Measurement-space ML reproduces the node to 0.95° / 32 mm**, same quaternion, same in-sample RMS (307 vs 298 mm) → the live extrinsic is confirmed.
- The residual gap is fully explained by (a) the logged `p_cam` being rounded to 3 dp and (b) the offline solve fixing the offset instead of jointly refining it.
- **Kabsch lands 16.6° away** — a live demonstration of *why* the tool uses measurement-space weighting rather than isotropic least squares (radar noise is anisotropic; Kabsch is biased by it).
