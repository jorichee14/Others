How to place a camera
            
Hold the board still where the reference camera (the ZED) and this camera both see it: green dots in both. Hold about a second, move it to another spot or angle, hold again. After 3 or more placements the camera is placed.

            
With the ZED pose server, the robot may move between placements, but keep it still while you hold the board.

How to record
          
Join with your name, then press Record. Move the board left, right, up and down (x, y), close and far (z), turn it in its plane (roll) and tilt it forward/back (pitch) and left/right (yaw). Bring it into the corners of the image too. A frame saves by itself each time the board has moved; move slowly to avoid blur.

          
When every bar is green and 20+ frames are saved, press Calibrate. Good: test error under 1 px (under 0.5 is very good).
