Calibration

How to recordJoin with your name and board, then Record on this camera. Move the board left, right, up, down (x y), close and far (z), turn it in its plane (roll), tilt it forward/back (pitch) and left/right (yaw). Frames save by themselves when the board has moved. When every bar is green and 20+ frames are saved, press Calibrate. Want test error under 1 px.

Frames

Map: corners of every saved frame (green calibrate, orange test); fill the whole image. Thumbnails: green circles found, red dots where the calibration puts them, error in px. Click to enlarge; ✕ deletes a bad frame (then Calibrate again).

Extrinsics

Every camera watches every joined board. Hold your board still for a second where two or more cameras see it; an event is saved and counted. Move, hold again. Cover every overlap. Aim for 150+ events in total.
