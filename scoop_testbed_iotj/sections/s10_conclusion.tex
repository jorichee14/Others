\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
SCooP-iT records a heterogeneous indoor team's sensing and every
inter-agent link on one clock, with measured offsets below
\SI{1}{\milli\second}, and in one metric frame, with the LiDAR robot
placed to \SI{10}{\milli\metre} against held-out boards and its scale
confirmed to about \SI{2}{\centi\metre} RMS by control distances,
without motion capture or GNSS. Its logged link replays per message into any
collaborative method, including unmodified ROS~2 systems,
\todo{reproducing live arrivals to within one frame}. Compared with a
synthetic channel fitted to the same statistics, the logged link
\todo{changes the predicted delivery of large messages, the best
send-size policy, and the behaviour of a collaborative SLAM system}. The
next step is a closed-loop environment built from the same capture, a
twin of the measured building with its measured link, in which agents
can act on what they receive and whose predictions can be checked on
the testbed. The pipeline and the replay module are released with this
paper, and the sequences and labels as the SCooP dataset
\cite{scoop_dataset}.
