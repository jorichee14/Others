\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
SCooP-iT records a heterogeneous indoor team's sensing and every
inter-agent link on one clock, with measured offsets below
\SI{1}{\milli\second}, and in one metric frame, with the LiDAR robot
placed to \SI{10}{\milli\metre} against held-out boards and its scale
confirmed to about \SI{2}{\centi\metre} RMS by control distances,
without motion capture or GNSS. Other researchers can test any collaborative method,
including unmodified ROS~2 systems, on the recordings through a replay
of the recorded link, \todo{whose predicted arrivals match
live messages to within one frame}. Compared with a
synthetic channel fitted to the same statistics, the logged link
\todo{changes the predicted delivery of large messages, the best
send-size policy, and the behaviour of a collaborative SLAM system}. A
digital twin built from the same capture with each part checked against
held-out passes, trains models that are then tested on held-out real
recordings \todo{result}. The next step closes the loop: motion
policies trained in the twin, deployed on the testbed, and their real
outcomes compared with the twin's predictions. The pipeline, the replay, and the twin are released with this
paper, and the sequences and labels as the SCooP dataset
\cite{scoop_dataset}.
