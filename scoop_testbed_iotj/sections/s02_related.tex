\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{IoT and Wireless Testbeds}
FIT IoT-LAB \cite{adjih2015iotlab} offers thousands of low-power nodes
and over a hundred mobile robots across indoor sites, POWDER
\cite{breen2021powder} and COSMOS \cite{raychaudhuri2020cosmos}
city-scale software-defined radio, and Colosseum
\cite{bonati2021colosseum} hardware emulation of the RF channel between
128 radios. They make the radio the object of study: positions come
from GNSS or the node itself, and no perception task is scored against
an independent reference.

\subsection{Trace-Driven Replay}
Recorded networks have been replayed for network software since trace
modulation \cite{noble1997trace}, through netem \cite{hemminger2005netem},
Cellsim \cite{winstein2013sprout}, Mahimahi \cite{netravali2015mahimahi},
and the calibrated emulators of Pantheon \cite{yan2018pantheon}. Wi-Fi is
the hard case: capacity measured by saturation depends on concurrent
traffic and on whether both directions are loaded at once
\cite{syed2020wifiemu}, and NemFi \cite{nemfi} reaches application
performance within a few percent of the live link only by modelling
aggregation and medium access. A replayed trace is also biased by the
traffic that produced it, so a policy that changes the offered load is
not evaluated correctly by replaying it \cite{causalsim}. These
emulators feed a socket; none replays a link into a multi-agent
perception method keyed to sensor time, and none is checked against the
arrivals such a method would see live.

\subsection{Channel Measurement}
Commodity CSI extraction \cite{halperin2011csitool,nexmon,jiang2022picoscenes}
made Wi-Fi a sensor \cite{ma2019wifisensing}. Channel datasets pair CSI
with positions from a tachymeter, total station, or GNSS
\cite{dichasus,espargos}, with human pose \cite{mmfi}, or with robot
arms observed by sniffers \cite{robomnist}, and ray tracing predicts the
channel from geometry \cite{hoydis2023sionnart,deepmimo}. CSI is
measured on sounding links or used to sense people, positions come from
a survey instrument, and geometry is an input to prediction rather than
an independent check on the measured channel.

\subsection{Multi-Robot Systems and Datasets with Real Links}
CHORD and ACHORD \cite{ginting2021chord,saboia2022achord} route data over
intermittent links in subterranean deployments, MOCHA
\cite{cladera2024mocha} synchronizes air--ground teams when the signal
allows, and ROS-NetSim \cite{calvofullana2021rosnetsim} passes ROS
traffic through a simulated network. The closest dataset is the
planetary-analogue C-SLAM dataset of \cite{planetary_cslam}: three
robots on an ad hoc network release LiDAR, IMU, odometry, and real-time
inter-robot throughput and latency, and Swarm-SLAM is studied under the
limited link. It is outdoor and homogeneous, carries no channel state,
scores no perception, and does not replay its link. Indoor multi-robot
datasets obtain their reference from motion capture, surveyed anchors,
or a scanner-built prior map \cite{utias,hilti2022,airmuseum,subtmrs}.

\subsection{Cooperative Perception}
Cooperative perception fuses raw points \cite{chen2019cooper}, features
\cite{chen2019fcooper,wang2020v2vnet}, or selected regions
\cite{where2comm}, and methods now target latency
\cite{lei2022syncnet,v2xvit}, asynchrony \cite{wei2023cobevflow}, and
loss \cite{ren2024v2xincop}, each evaluated against a constant delay, a
sampled lag, or a Bernoulli drop. EMP \cite{zhang2021emp} adapts what
vehicles share to a fluctuating edge link. Real-world platforms are
outdoor and GNSS-referenced: V2V4Real \cite{v2v4real} and TUMTraf-V2X
\cite{zimmer2024tumtrafv2x} log no link, V2X-ReaLO \cite{v2xrealo} runs
online over a live link, and CooperScene \cite{cooperscene}, the nearest
case, releases pairwise C-V2X latency, throughput, loss, and jitter with
PTP-synchronized sensors and benchmarks detection under the measured
throughput. CoPeD \cite{coped} records an air--ground team indoors and
outdoors with no link log.

\subsection{Simulators and Digital Twins}
Embodied agents are trained in simulators that generate their world,
from Isaac Lab \cite{orbit} to city-scale multi-agent platforms whose
messages pass an ideal bus \cite{lingjing}; ns3-gym \cite{ns3gym} exposes
a simulated network to learning. Twins of real testbeds are checked
against them: Colosseum reproduces an indoor wireless testbed's
throughput and SINR \cite{villa2024colosseumdt}, and for navigation, how
well simulation predicts real results is measured by the correlation of
sim and real scores across policies \cite{kadian2020sim2real}. No twin
reproduces a multi-agent team's sensing and link together and is checked
against held-out real passes before it trains anything.

\subsection{The Gap}

measure the radio without a perception task; replay systems are
validated for sockets, not for multi-agent methods; channel datasets
rest on survey instruments; robot datasets with real links are outdoor
and carry no channel state or replay; and cooperative-perception
platforms are outdoor and use the link as a scalar or a live run.
