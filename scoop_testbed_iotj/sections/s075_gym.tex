\section{SCooP-iT Gym: A Twin Built from the Recordings}
\label{sec:gym}
Recordings answer what happened; they cannot show what an agent would
have sensed or sent along a route it never took, with more people in
the corridor, or with a camera that failed. SCooP-iT Gym is a digital
twin built from the recordings for those questions, in which data can
be generated at will and methods trained, with one rule that keeps it
tied to the testbed: nothing trained in the twin is evaluated in the
twin. Passes are split into build passes, from which the twin is made,
and held-out passes from another day, which are used only to check the
twin and to test what is trained in it (Fig.~\ref{fig:gym}).

\paragraph{The world.} Each part is built from the build passes, and
each is tagged by where it comes from. \emph{Measured}: the building,
the reference map of Sec.~\ref{sec:gt} exported to Isaac Sim
\cite{isaacsim} as a surface in the map frame; the sensors, with the
real LiDAR beam patterns, camera intrinsics, and the mounts of
Table~\ref{tab:calib}; the recorded paths; the link, logged or fitted
along those paths (Sec.~\ref{sec:replay}), and indexed by place for new
routes, from the survey's measurements per map cell and the contention
measured between survey and cooperation passes; and sensor dropouts
and pose error, fitted from the recordings. \emph{Added}: new routes,
people, objects, and lighting, placed in the map's free space at the
class counts of the recordings, each with an exact box at every stamp.
A scenario chooses a setting per part, and any number of scenarios are
generated.

\paragraph{The gate.} Before any part is used for training, it is
checked against the held-out passes: synthetic scans at the recorded
poses against the real scans, the fitted dropouts against the dropouts
of a held-out session, and the place-indexed link against the logged
link of a held-out pass along that pass's own path (E6a). A part that
fails is not used to train; a failed link, for example, falls back to
the logged or fitted link on the recorded paths.

\paragraph{Train in the twin, test on held-out recordings.} Perception
and communication models, a cooperative detector, a fusion method, or a
policy for what to send, are trained on twin scenarios and tested on the
held-out real recordings through the replay, with the logged link and
real labels (E6b). Motion cannot be tested on a recording; a motion
policy trained in the twin is tested by deploying it on the testbed,
the step after this paper.

\paragraph{Interface.} Agents reset and step through the Gymnasium and
PettingZoo interfaces \cite{gymnasium,pettingzoo} on Isaac Lab
\cite{orbit}; the replay of Sec.~\ref{sec:replay} decides which
messages arrive, and every miss is logged with its cause (outage,
queue, transmission, or latency). Unlike simulators that generate their
world and pass messages over an ideal bus \cite{lingjing}, and unlike
network gyms over a simulated network \cite{ns3gym}, every part here is
measured or checked against the place it was measured.
