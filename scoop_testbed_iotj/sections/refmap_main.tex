% Sec. V-A "Reference Map" body for main.tex, with the bundle-adjusted and
% TSDF-fused reference (pass 20261015). \input from main.tex. Full method
% and equations: supplementary, supp/refmap_method.tex.
The reference map is built in a dedicated mapping session and held fixed
afterwards; no recorded run contributes to it. A second, separate mapping
pass is recorded and processed independently and is used only to
cross-check the first. A LiDAR-inertial SLAM solution \cite{glim} gives the
initial trajectory; its poses are stamped \SI{46}{\milli\second} early,
which, measured against the scans and corrected, removes most of its
heading error (supplementary material). It is refined by plane
bundle adjustment \cite{balm,hba}: the poses of \SI{1}{\second} keyframes
are moved jointly so that every planar patch seen from several keyframes is
as thin as possible, the cost of a patch being the smallest eigenvalue of
the scatter of its points, with the SLAM solution's relative motion and
gravity direction kept as soft priors. The scans are then fused into a
truncated signed distance field \cite{curless1996,vdbfusion} with
\SI{2}{\centi\metre} cells and a truncation of \SI{8}{\centi\metre}, about
three standard deviations of the spread with which the scans place a wall;
the map is its zero-level surface, and moving objects are removed by
free-space carving \cite{dufomap}. Fusion matters as much as the poses: the
usual union of points keeps every cell a stray point reached, so its walls
(\SI{12.7}{\centi\metre}) are thicker than the scans that built them
(\SI{9.7}{\centi\metre} over the run), whereas the signed distance averages
across a surface (\SI{7.6}{\centi\metre}). Each stage is measured by the
thickness of walls as the scans place them (stage by stage in the supplementary material):
over the whole run it goes from \SI{18.8}{\centi\metre} for the SLAM
solution to \SI{12.0}{\centi\metre} with the time correction and
\SI{9.7}{\centi\metre} with bundle adjustment, near the
\SI{6.9}{\centi\metre} the scans of one second reach; the final map's
walls and floors are \SI{7.6}{\centi\metre} and \SI{1.8}{\centi\metre}
thick. The board loop closure of the reference, \SI{12.3}{\milli\metre},
is within twice its detection noise, so no drift is detected over the run.
Per-scan registration against the SLAM map, the usual alternative,
agrees with the reference to \SI{1.3}{\centi\metre} and $0.15^\circ$
ATE, so the result is fixed by the scans rather than by the method.
