\section{Replay of the Logged Link}
\label{sec:replay}
This section describes how a new method is run against the link the
agents had. Whether the replay is faithful is tested in E3.

\paragraph{Model.} A message of size $s$ sent at time $t$ from agent $a$
to agent $b$ through the access point crosses two hops, $a$'s uplink and
$b$'s downlink, and arrives at
\begin{equation}
  t + \tfrac{1}{2}\big(\mathrm{RTT}_a(t) + \mathrm{RTT}_b(t)\big)
    + s\Big(\frac{1}{G^{\uparrow}_a(t)} + \frac{1}{G^{\downarrow}_b(t)}\Big),
  \label{eq:replay}
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{RTT}$ is the most recent round trip measured outside the
agent's own active test and $G$ the goodput of its most recent test in
that direction. A message sent during an outage, a run of unanswered
pings of at least one frame, is dropped. Round trips stand in for the
one-way delays until the payload stamps of E3 measure them: on a
contended link the two directions are not symmetric, and the strict
\SI{12}{\milli\second} clock bound is larger than an idle one-way delay,
so the clocks alone cannot resolve it.

\paragraph{Channels.} Four channels pass through one module, so the
channel is the only variable between runs of a method. \emph{Ideal}:
instant and lossless. \emph{Published}: the channels of prior benchmarks
as configured, a constant \SI{100}{\milli\second} (V2X-ViT \cite{v2xvit}),
a bandwidth budget without delay (Where2comm \cite{where2comm}), or a
Binomial frame lag (CoBEVFlow \cite{wei2023cobevflow}). \emph{Fitted}: a
synthetic channel per link with the logged link's statistics, a
lognormal latency, its median goodput, and two-state outages with the
measured share of time and mean length, but no tie to time or place.
\emph{Logged}: Eq.~\eqref{eq:replay}. The fitted channel is the
comparison that matters: any difference between it and the logged
channel comes from structure that only a measurement carries, such as
contention between agents and outages tied to places.

\paragraph{Interfaces.} A method that emits (sender, receiver, $t$,
size) receives an arrival time or a drop. Unmodified ROS~2 systems run
through a relay: each agent runs in its own ROS domain, and the relay
carries the topics one agent publishes and another subscribes to,
holding each serialized message until the channel releases it, on the
recording's clock. An optional queue makes back-to-back messages on a
link wait for each other.

\paragraph{Assumptions and limits.} Eq.~\eqref{eq:replay} is a fluid
model: it ignores frame aggregation and medium-access dynamics, which
NemFi \cite{nemfi} shows matter for faithful Wi-Fi replay, and its
goodput comes from saturating tests, whose result depends on concurrent
traffic \cite{syed2020wifiemu}. The trace was recorded under that
saturating load, so it describes a loaded channel, and a method's
messages are taken to replace part of that load rather than add to it; a
policy whose offered load exceeds the recorded one is outside what a
replayed trace can evaluate \cite{causalsim}. E3 measures the error that
remains.
